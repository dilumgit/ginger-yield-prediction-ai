6. ENGINEERED IN-SEASON FEATURES & AGRONOMIC JUSTIFICATIONS
   - Total_Water_Input_mm:
     * Formula: Weekly_Rainfall_mm + Irrigation_mm_week
     * Agronomic Rationale: Net combined water supplied via natural rainfall and supplemental irrigation.
     * In-Season Availability: Available at observation date.

   - Plant_Height_per_DAP:
     * Formula: Plant_Height_cm / Days_After_Planting
     * Agronomic Rationale: Daily vertical elongation velocity (cm/day), capturing early vegetative vigor.
     * In-Season Availability: Available at observation date.

   - Canopy_Greenness_Volume:
     * Formula: Plant_Height_cm * Leaf_Greenness_Index
     * Agronomic Rationale: Composite proxy for active photosynthetic canopy biomass and chlorophyll content.
     * In-Season Availability: Available at observation date.

   - Biotic_Stress_Index:
     * Formula: Pest_Severity_Ordinal + Disease_Severity_Ordinal (Range: 0 to 4)
     * Agronomic Rationale: Quantifies cumulative biotic stress pressure from insect pests and pathogens.
     * In-Season Availability: Available at observation date.

   - Planting_Month:
     * Formula: Extraction of calendar month from Planting_Date
     * Agronomic Rationale: Captures planting timing relative to Sri Lankan monsoonal seasons (Yala / Maha).
     * In-Season Availability: Known at planting.
